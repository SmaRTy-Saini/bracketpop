Privacy

BracketPop is a static website. It doesn't require an account and the application does not send your bracket entries or choices to an application database.

Sharing a link includes the bracket title, contenders, and potentially your picks in its URL. Anyone who receives that URL can view the information. Do not put private information in a bracket.

GitHub Pages, your browser, and any websites you visit through external links may process technical information according to their own privacy practices. This app does not claim that hosting infrastructure logs no data.

We do not currently offer paid subscriptions or user accounts.
